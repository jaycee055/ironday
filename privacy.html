Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version

IRONDAY works without an account. Everything you enter is saved on your own device. If you choose to create an account, your workout data is also backed up online so you can use it on other devices. There is no tracking and no advertising.

What the app stores

  • Your name and, if you choose to enter it, your email address
  • Your workout programs, completed tasks, workout history and settings
  • Body weights you log
  • Progress photos you add (shrunk to a small size first)

All of this is saved on your device (in the browser's storage, or inside the Android app). Without an account, it never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

Optional accounts and online backup

An account is optional. If you create one:

  • Your name, email address and a scrambled form of your password (a salted, repeatedly hashed code, never the password itself) are stored in a Google Sheet owned by the IRONDAY developer.
  • Your workout data (profile, programs, completed tasks, history, settings and logged weights) is backed up as a file in the IRONDAY developer's Google Drive, so you can sign in on another device.
  • If you use Forgot password, a one-time 6-digit code is emailed to your account address from the IRONDAY developer's Gmail. The code expires after 15 minutes. Your email is not used for anything else, such as newsletters.
  • Progress photos are never uploaded. They stay only on the device where you added them.
  • The developer can technically open these files. They are used only to run your account and are never sold or shared.
  • Google stores this data on its servers. See Google's privacy policy.

Photos

When you add a progress photo you pick it from your device. The app never uses your camera directly. Photos stay on your device and are only shown inside the app.

What is loaded from the internet

  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and the Android app is downloaded from GitHub. GitHub may log basic technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser loads the site. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • If you have an account, your device talks to the IRONDAY account server (Google Apps Script) to sign in and back up your data.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address when the fonts load. See Google's privacy policy.

After the first visit, the app caches its files so it works offline.

Analytics, ads and sharing

None. IRONDAY contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers, and it does not sell or share your data.

Deleting your data

Open Profile → Reset all data to delete your programs, progress, weights, history and photos (and, if you are signed in, your online backup). Open Profile → Delete account to delete your account and your online backup; data on your device stays until you reset it or remove the app. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the app removes everything stored on that device.

Children

IRONDAY is a general fitness app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health notice

The workouts are general information, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have an injury or health condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
